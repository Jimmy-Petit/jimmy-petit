From the 21st to the 25th of September 2026, CuttingGardens 2026 took place, focusing specifically on EEG processing. At the Würzburg garden, which had a Star Wars theme, I was invited to give a workshop  on EEG preprocessing -- Thank you! Maria and Prof. Kübler! -- , more specifically on filtering. The garden's objective was to focus on the fundamental tools of preprocessing, as well as new tools, and especially to explored the hidden assumptions and costs involved.
